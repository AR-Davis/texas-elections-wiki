The $3 Million Pre-Trial Payment

Three days before Paxton's impeachment trial, Defend Texas Liberty PAC gave:

  • $1 million in campaign donations to Lt. Gov. Dan Patrick
  • $2 million forgivable loan to Patrick

Thirty times more than what the PAC gave Patrick in 2022 when he was actually running for reelection.

Patrick's duties: Preside as judge in Paxton's Senate impeachment trial.

Result: Patrick acquitted Paxton on all 16 articles and immediately attacked House leadership for wasting time.

"This tirade made it clear the fix had been in from the moment Patrick grabbed the gavel." — Rep. Ann Johnson (impeachment manager)

The Nick Fuentes Scandal & Rebranding

October 2023: Texas Tribune reveals Jonathan Stickland met with Nick Fuentes (Holocaust denier, white supremacist) for hours at Wilks Development property.

Immediate aftermath:

  • Stickland "replaced" as PAC president
  • PAC rebranded as "Texans United for a Conservative Majority"
  • Funding continued: Dunn immediately gave $700K to new PAC; Wilks gave $1.29M
  • New logo: Goddess of Liberty statue replaced with a Christian cross

Why Dunn Chose Paxton

Tim Dunn didn't randomly choose to fund Ken Paxton. He cultivated Paxton from his first race, recognizing that a statewide AG could advance Dunn's Christian nationalist agenda while protecting Dunn's other investments.

  • Ethics Commission Attack: Paxton, as AG, refused to defend the Texas Ethics Commission against lawsuits filed by Empower Texans (Dunn's group). This left the campaign finance watchdog toothless.
  • Campaign Finance Gutting: Paxton effectively eliminated enforcement of Texas campaign finance laws, making Dunn's unlimited spending easier to hide.
  • Christian Nationalist Policies: Paxton advanced Dunn's agenda: sued to end DACA, attacked abortion providers, fought LGBTQ+ rights, challenged federal environmental regulations.
  • National Platform: With Paxton as US Senator, Dunn gains access to national security intelligence, federal prosecutorial influence, and a platform to export the "Texas model" nationally.

Why "Dark Money"

501(c)(4) organizations (Texas Family Project, Defend Texas Liberty "Foundation") don't have to disclose:

  • Who gave the money
  • How it's spent
  • What the true objectives are

Result: We only see the disclosed PAC money. The actual total could be double or triple the $14M we can trace.
