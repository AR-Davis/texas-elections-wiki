The Six Operations

1. Texas Public Policy Foundation (TPPF) — Policy Creation. Dunn: Vice Chairman since 1998. The 42,000 sq ft "Kremlin on Congress Avenue" drafts model legislation.

2. Pipeline Media — Disguised Amplification. 1,200+ "local news" websites that look independent but churn out anti-renewable energy "stories" and attacks on disobedient Republicans.

3. Texas Scorecard — Attack Operations. Publishes 0-100 "fiscal responsibility" scores. Low scorers face funded primary challenges. Former Rep Bennett Ratliff called it "flat extortion, not a thumb on the scale."

4. Defend Texas Liberty PAC — Financial Enforcement. $14.5M from Dunn + Wilks. Funds primary challengers. Nick Fuentes scandal: Stickland met with white supremacist at Wilks property.

5. Texas Family Project — Direct Voter Manipulation. Sends false text messages, anti-Muslim mailers, dark money "voter guides."

6. Ballotpedia — Voter Information Control. "Ultra-local" initiative fills news deserts with Dunn-influenced information. Poses as nonpartisan.
